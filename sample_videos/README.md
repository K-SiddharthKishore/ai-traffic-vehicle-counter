# Sample Videos

Place your test traffic videos here (MP4, MOV, or AVI format).

## Good sources for free traffic footage
- Pixabay (pixabay.com) — search "traffic" and filter for video
- Pexels (pexels.com/videos) — search "traffic road"
- Your own mobile camera recording of a road from a fixed position

## Tips for best results
- Record from a fixed position (overhead or side angle works best)
- Vehicles should clearly cross a horizontal or vertical line
- 10–60 second clips are ideal for testing
- Good lighting and at least 480p resolution recommended
